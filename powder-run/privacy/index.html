Powder Run Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Powder Run ("the game") does not collect your personal information, and it does not require an account, show ads or track you. This policy explains what the game stores on your device and what it does not do. It applies to the Powder Run mobile app on Google Play and the Apple App Store, made by Wyatt Lewin ("I", "me").

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in local storage on your device so they are there next time you play. This data stays on your device and is not sent to me or to anyone else. It includes:

  • Progress: distance records, snowflakes earned, ramps jumped, and the scenery, tricks and costumes you have unlocked
  • Choices: your selected ski type, costume and customizations, and which collection items are switched on
  • Settings: steering style and tuning, camera distance, sensitivity, sound, and display options

This data does not identify you. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

What I do not collect or share

The game does not collect, store on a server, sell or share:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details (there are no accounts)
  • Your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data
  • Advertising identifiers, device identifiers or analytics about how you play

The game contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no social-media tracking. It does not use cookies for tracking. Because I collect no personal information, there is nothing to sell, rent or disclose to third parties.

Purchases. The game has no in-app purchases. Payment for the download is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. I receive no payment details from you.

Third-party stores. Google and Apple may collect information about your device and downloads under their own policies: the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Powder Run does not request device permissions beyond what the operating system grants every app by default.

Children's privacy

The game is suitable for a general audience, including children, because it collects no personal information from anyone. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age), and the game has no chat, user-generated content, accounts or links that lead children to share information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to me, contact me and I will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You control everything the game stores. To delete your saved progress and settings, clear the game's data in your device settings or uninstall the game.

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct, port or delete. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you have rights over your personal data under laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA. You can still contact me to ask about your data, and I will reply within the time the law requires. I do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined in California law.

Changes to this policy

If I change how the game handles data, for example by adding ads, analytics or accounts, I will update this policy and its effective date before the change ships, and I will tell you in the app or on the store listing where the law requires it.
