Powder Run Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Powder Run is a skiing game for Android made by Wyatt Lewin ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game handles and how. The short version: Powder Run doesn't collect, store or share your personal information.

Information the game collects

Powder Run does not collect personal information. It does not ask you to create an account and does not request your name, email address, contacts, location or photos.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings, unlocked scenery and skier customizations on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data stays on your device. I can't see it, and it is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

Third-party services

Powder Run does not currently include advertising, analytics or other third-party services that collect data. If that changes, this policy will be updated to name each service and what it collects, before the change ships.

Google Play, which distributes the app, may collect information under its own policies when you download or update the game. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Powder Run is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or anyone else.

Permissions

Powder Run does not request device permissions beyond what Android grants every app by default.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
