01About

I'm a senior program manager who likes the unglamorous part of making things: turning a messy, half-formed idea into a plan a team can actually execute.

I started in social and community at HTC during the early Android years. From there I moved to technical program management at Tableau, then ran program management for Alexa Developer Experience at Amazon. At IQVIA I built a Project Management Organization from scratch and led a team of Design Program Managers. Most recently I worked on Google Beam.

I'm comfortable on the technical side too. I write Kotlin, Lua, Python, JavaScript and shell, and I build my own tools when the off-the-shelf ones don't fit. A few of those tools have turned into the projects below.
